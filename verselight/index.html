Bible translation

Two translations, and Verselight moves between them on its own. With a signal you read the English Standard Version. Without one you read the King James Version — all 66 books, 31,102 verses, already stored on this device.

That split is a licensing boundary, not a technical one. The KJV is public domain worldwide, which is what lets Verselight be passed phone to phone and stay legal at every hop. Crossway’s ESV API is free for non-commercial use but caps local storage at 500 verses, so the ESV cannot live on the device — it is fetched as you read and never written to storage.

Nothing is required of you. If you would rather the requests count against your own Crossway account, put your key here — get one at api.esv.org. Leave it empty to use Verselight’s.
